## An Important Update

After careful consideration, we've made the decision to temporarily take our web application offline as we reimagine the future of SciMigo.

## What's Changing?

Over the past months, we've learned a tremendous amount from our users and the evolving landscape of AI-powered education. While our mission remains the same—making high-quality STEM education accessible to everyone—we're taking a step back to refocus our approach.

**Current Status:**
- Our web application (app.scimigo.com) is currently offline
- The Chrome Extension remains available for now
- We're exploring new directions for AI curriculum generation and personalized learning experiences

## Our New Vision

We're shifting our focus to:

- **AI Curriculum Generation**: Creating personalized, adaptive learning paths tailored to each student's needs
- **Interactive Learning Experiences**: Building more engaging ways to explore STEM concepts
- **Personalized Education**: Moving beyond simple problem-solving to comprehensive learning journeys

Rather than maintaining a traditional web application, we're exploring how AI can fundamentally transform how students learn and engage with STEM subjects.

## What This Means for You

While we work on this new direction:
- Our Chrome Extension continues to provide instant help on any webpage
- We're actively developing new features and approaches
- This is a pivot, not an ending—we're more committed than ever to our mission

## Looking Forward

This decision wasn't made lightly. However, we believe that taking this time to reimagine our approach will allow us to create something truly transformative in AI-powered education.

We're excited about what's ahead and can't wait to share more as we develop this new direction.

## Stay Connected

We'll keep you updated on our progress through this blog. If you have thoughts, ideas, or just want to follow along with our journey, stay tuned for future updates.

Thank you for your support and understanding as we work to create the future of AI-powered STEM education.
