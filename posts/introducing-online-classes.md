## A New Chapter for SciMigo

Today, we're excited to announce the next evolution of SciMigo: **AI-powered online math classes** that combine cutting-edge technology with personalized education.

After years of developing educational content and AI tutoring systems, we're bringing everything together into a comprehensive learning platform that adapts to each student's needs.

## What We're Building

### Interactive Mini-Lectures

Our proprietary **Mini Lecture Pipeline** automatically generates complete educational experiences:

- **Professional slide presentations** with synchronized narration
- **LaTeX-rendered equations** for mathematical precision
- **Fragment-by-fragment explanations** for progressive comprehension
- **Visual diagrams and animations** for intuitive understanding

Each lecture is crafted by AI but designed with pedagogical principles developed through years of teaching experience.

### Your Personal AI Tutor

Learning doesn't stop at watching lectures. When you're stuck, our **intelligent tutoring system** provides instant, personalized help:

- **Context-aware assistance** based on the current lecture content
- **Multiple learning modes**: Choose hints, full solutions, or step-by-step tutorials
- **Natural conversation interface** that understands math problems
- **Available 24/7** whenever you need help

The AI adapts its explanations to your learning style and the specific concept you're working on.

### Adaptive Curriculum

Content that grows with you:

- **Structured learning paths** from fundamentals to advanced topics
- **Progress tracking** to monitor your achievements
- **Personalized recommendations** for what to study next
- **Self-paced** so you can master each concept thoroughly

## Our First Course: Discrete Mathematics

We're launching with **Discrete Mathematics Foundations**, a 12-session course covering:

- Sets, Relations & Functions
- Logic & Proof Techniques
- Combinatorics & Counting
- Graph Theory Fundamentals
- Number Theory Basics
- Recursion & Induction
- Algorithm Analysis
- Discrete Probability

This course is designed for students preparing for computer science studies, competitive programming, or anyone wanting to strengthen their mathematical reasoning skills.

## The Technology Behind It

SciMigo is built on advanced AI technology we've developed and refined:

### Mini Lecture Pipeline

Our system automatically generates complete lectures from topic descriptions:
- Structured lesson specifications with validated schemas
- Professional Reveal.js presentations with offline KaTeX support
- High-quality text-to-speech narration with voice customization

### Intelligent Tutoring System

Multi-agent AI architecture that:
- Routes problems to specialized mathematical solvers

- Adapts explanations to individual learning styles
- Provides step-by-step reasoning with LaTeX formatting
