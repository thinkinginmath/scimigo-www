## The Story Behind SciMigo

My journey to creating SciMigo began with a passion for making mathematics accessible to everyone. For years, I've been running the [Thinking in Math YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC5uV1LcSkHL5zCxKBikdTEg), where I break down complex mathematical concepts into digestible explanations. Through this platform, I've had the privilege of helping countless students discover the beauty and logic behind mathematical thinking.

Beyond YouTube, I've spent significant time tutoring high schoolers preparing for competitive mathematics competitions, as well as college students tackling discrete mathematics and algorithms. Each tutoring session reinforced a fundamental truth: **every student learns differently**. Some need step-by-step breakdowns, others benefit from hints that guide them to discover solutions themselves, and many require connecting new concepts to familiar foundations.

However, traditional tutoring has limitations. As much as I wanted to help every student who reached out, there simply weren't enough hours in the day. That's when I realized the transformative potential of AI in education.

- **Covers multiple subjects** - From calculus to algorithms, all in one place

This vision drove me to create SciMigo - not just another AI tool, but a comprehensive educational companion designed with the insights gained from years of teaching and tutoring experience.

## What Makes SciMigo Different?

Unlike traditional AI assistants, SciMigo uses a sophisticated **multi-agent architecture** that intelligently routes your problems to specialized AI agents. Whether you're struggling with calculus derivatives, debugging code, or understanding graph algorithms, our system automatically selects the best agent for your specific problem.

### Our Specialized Agents

- **Math Specialists**: Dedicated agents for calculus, algebra, differential equations, and linear algebra
- **Computer Science Experts**: Specialized in data structures, algorithm analysis, and programming concepts  
- **Educational Tutors**: Focused on pedagogical explanations that help you understand the "why" behind solutions

## Learning Modes Inspired by Real Tutoring

Drawing from my tutoring experience, I've built SciMigo with multiple learning modes that mirror how I adapt my teaching approach for different students:

- **Hints Mode**: Just like when I guide competitive math students to discover solutions themselves, this mode provides progressive hints that build problem-solving confidence without giving away the answer
- **Solve Mode**: Perfect for when students need to see the complete methodology - detailed step-by-step solutions with explanations, similar to my YouTube video breakdowns
- **Tutor Mode**: Inspired by my discrete math and algorithms tutoring sessions, this mode focuses on the "why" behind each step, connecting new concepts to fundamental principles
- **Multi-Agent Mode**: Like having access to multiple subject specialists, our system automatically routes problems to the most appropriate AI agent

Each mode reflects a different teaching scenario I've encountered - from the high schooler who needs to build confidence through discovery, to the college student who needs comprehensive understanding of complex algorithms.
