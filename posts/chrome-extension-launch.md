We're thrilled to announce that the **SciMigo Chrome Extension** is now officially available on the Chrome Web Store! After extensive beta testing and refinement, we're ready to bring AI-powered STEM tutoring directly to your browser.

## What is the SciMigo Chrome Extension?

The SciMigo Chrome Extension transforms any webpage into a personalized learning opportunity. Whether you're browsing Stack Overflow, reading through Khan Academy, working on LeetCode problems, or studying from Wikipedia math articles, you can now get instant AI tutoring help with just a highlight and click.

### Key Features

🔍 **Universal Compatibility**: Works on any webpage across the internet
🧮 **Math Support**: Get step-by-step explanations for equations, formulas, and mathematical concepts  
💻 **Code Analysis**: Understand algorithms, data structures, and programming concepts
🎯 **Context-Aware**: Our AI understands the context of what you're highlighting
⚡ **Instant Results**: No page redirects - get help right where you are
📚 **Multiple Learning Modes**: Choose from hints, full solutions, or conceptual explanations

## How It Works

Using the extension is incredibly simple:

1. **Highlight** any math equation, code snippet, or STEM-related text on any webpage
2. **Click** the SciMigo button that appears near your selection
3. **Choose** your preferred learning mode (hints, solve, or tutor)
4. **Learn** with personalized, step-by-step AI explanations

Our multi-agent AI system automatically routes your highlighted content to the most appropriate specialist - whether that's our math agents for calculus problems or our computer science agents for algorithm analysis.
